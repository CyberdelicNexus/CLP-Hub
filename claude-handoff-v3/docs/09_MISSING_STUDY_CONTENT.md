# Missing study content

These items do not block visual prototyping, but required items block production publication.

## Recruitment and protocol

- Public Spanish study title
- Target population and approved participant description
- Minimum/maximum age
- Country, cities and participating sites
- Recruitment start/end dates and current status
- Total study duration and participant time commitment
- Exact number and format of study visits/sessions
- Approved description of the program group
- Approved description of the control group
- Randomization wording and any allocation ratio
- Inclusion criteria
- Exclusion criteria
- Possible benefits
- Risks, discomforts and participant burdens
- Withdrawal and contact procedure
- Costs, reimbursement or compensation
- Equipment provision, setup and return arrangements
- Accessibility/accommodation details

## Governance and contact

- Principal investigator
- Sponsor and responsible institution
- Ethics body and approval/reference
- Public registry and study identifier
- Recruitment-material version/date
- Study email and telephone
- Approved alternative contact route

## Links and media

- Production Qualtrics URL
- Approved privacy link
- Approved study-information link
- Approved Spanish captions, voiceover or transcript for the Section 3 film
- Confirmation of which supplied phase clips may be published
- Confirmation of public-use rights for generated participants and Quest 3 product depiction
- Final logo files, preferably SVG

## Copy approval

- Native Spanish editorial review
- Clinical review of mortality and serious-illness language
- Ethics/legal approval of recruitment copy
- Approval of stage descriptions
- Approval of group labels
