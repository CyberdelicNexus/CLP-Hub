Audience sequencing

A hierarchy is needed; the data does not provide one

The team names several valid audiences but no shared launch audience. The sequence below is a working strategic hypothesis.

01 / LAUNCH WEDGE

Contemplatively curious participants

People already seeking meaningful, embodied ways to explore connection, perspective and consciousness—and willing to enter with preparation.

Start with readiness, not demographics.
02 / TRUST & REACH

Facilitators and practice leaders

People who add context, continuity, community and responsible distribution around the experience.

Build the bridge from solo interest to shared practice.
03 / VALIDATION

Researchers and care partners

Institutions that generate evidence, define appropriate applications and extend access where safeguards are strongest.

Do not let the clinical use case define the entire brand.
04 / ECOSYSTEM GROWTH

Experience creators

Advanced practitioners and designers who can expand the field once tools, review standards and economics are ready.

A later growth engine, not the first public promise.

03 / WORKING RECOMMENDATION

What the brand should do next

Make the invisible
experientially clear.

This is a strategic first draft built from six internal perspectives. It is intentionally decisive enough to test—and explicitly provisional where research has not produced a mandate.

Recommended brand platform / v0.1

From knowing
to feeling.

The organising idea is the gap between intellectually understanding connection, impermanence or boundlessness—and experiencing them as felt realities.

Vision
A world where technology nurtures attention instead of extracting it.
Purpose
Make profound states of connection and perspective more accessible without stripping them of context, care or mystery.
Mission
Design, study and responsibly distribute numadelic practices that help people move from knowing to feeling—alone and together.
Brand promise
An invitation to experience what words alone cannot reach.

Positioning / working draft

A clear sentence before a clever slogan

For contemplatively curious people and the practitioners who guide them, aNUma is an evidence-informed immersive practice platform that makes insight and connection felt through carefully designed numadelic experiences—solo and shared. Unlike entertainment-first VR or passive meditation apps, it combines an original embodied aesthetic, responsible preparation and scientific inquiry.
CLAIM GUARDRAIL

Offer conditions for insight, connection and self-transcendent experience. Never promise healing, treatment, guaranteed transformation or equivalence to psychedelics.

Five operating principles

The brand must behave before it decorates

01

Depth over spectacle

Use sensory restraint to focus attention. Never add novelty that competes with the experience’s meaning.

02

Invitation over prescription

Create curiosity and agency. Avoid preaching, dogma and claims about what someone should experience.

03

Evidence without sterility

Make the science visible and legible while protecting warmth, poetry and first-person testimony.

04

Context is part of the product

Preparation, consent, facilitation and integration are not support content; they are core experience design.

05

Technology serves attention

Use VR only where immersion, embodiment or remote co-presence materially improves the practice.

Product architecture

Three pathways. One continuity layer.

Descriptive names are placeholders for validation. The structural recommendation is the important part.

01 / EXPLORE

Solo journeys

A prepared, bounded entry into numadelic practice—not an endless content library.

Preparation → experience → integration → invitation onward
02 / GATHER

Guided practice

Facilitator-led group experiences, training and session tools that protect the relational core.

Community → facilitation → evidence → repeat practice
03 / CREATE

Experience studio

Tools and standards for serious creators to build, review and publish numadelic experiences.

Authoring → review → permissions → distribution
CONTINUITY LAYERPrepare · Consent · Reflect · Integrate · Belong · Learn

Public narrative sequence

What the first 30 seconds must accomplish

  1. 01
    Name the felt gap

    You can understand connection intellectually and still not feel it.

  2. 02
    Explain aNUma plainly

    Immersive practices designed to make perspective and connection experientially available.

  3. 03
    Earn permission to go deeper

    Show the research, the lineage, the original aesthetic and the people behind the work.

  4. 04
    Offer one legible doorway

    Start with a prepared solo experience or find a facilitated gathering.

Brand-to-product roadmap

Sequence confidence before scale

NOW / 0–3 MONTHS

Make the doorway legible

  • Resolve entity and audience decisions
  • Test positioning with target participants
  • Prototype solo preparation and integration
  • Build a proof and testimonial library
NEXT / 3–9 MONTHS

Prove responsible access

  • Pilot the prepared solo pathway
  • Formalise facilitator training and network
  • Test pricing by context, not one universal model
  • Measure use, safety, return and continuity
LATER / 9–18 MONTHS

Open the ecosystem carefully

  • Release creator tools to a reviewed cohort
  • Define publishing and quality standards
  • Expand the companion and citizen-research layer
  • Explore non-VR numadelic formats

Method and limits

How to read this analysis

01

Findings describe repeated or divergent response patterns. Closed-choice counts are calculated directly from the six exports.

02

Interpretations are reasoned hypotheses about what those patterns mean. They are deliberately labelled and should be challenged.

03

Recommendations apply strategic judgment. They are not automatically produced by majority vote.

04

This is a small internal sample, not market research. Qualitative strength reflects recurrence, specificity and relevance—not statistical significance.

05

Imported strategist answers stay separate from team counts so post-hoc influence does not rewrite the original evidence.
